Ask an NBA question. Get the answer and the data under it.

Dime is an analyst for NBA data. It runs the queries, shows them, and checks every figure.

Compare Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Luka Doncic over their last 30 games.
  • Pulled 60 game logs from 2025-26
  • Computed per-game and per-100 rates
  • Checked both totals against box scores

Shai scores more on fewer shots. He leads by 2.1 points per game and 5.8 points of true shooting. Luka creates more for others, with 2.4 more assists per game, and carries the larger share of his team's possessions.

Features

  • Matchups

    Put two players or teams side by side over any span you name.

  • Lineups

    Rank five-man units by minutes, net rating and shot profile.

  • Trades

    Check salary, picks and roster fit before the deadline does.

  • Awards

    See how a season's voting case compares with past winners.

  • Props

    Count how often a line has hit in situations like tonight's.

How it works
  1. 1

    Ask

    Type the question the way you would ask a colleague.

  2. 2

    Dime queries the data

    It plans the lookups, runs them on the warehouse and shows each one.

  3. 3

    Every number is checked

    Figures that don't match a source row don't make it into the answer.
