Question

How do the Thunder and the Celtics match up this season?

Checked four factors for both teams.

Boston is better in the half court. Oklahoma City is better everywhere else. The Thunder rebound more, run faster, and score 4.7 points per 100 possessions more in transition. Boston shoots better and turns it over about a point less often, which nets 3.2 points per 100 possessions on the other side.

Features

What you can ask

Matchups
Compare two teams on pace, shooting, rebounding, and turnovers.
Lineups
Find the units that play best with or without a given player.
Trades
See which side a trade helps and what it costs the other club.
Awards
Rank players on the numbers behind MVP and All-NBA voting.
Props
Count the times a player cleared a line at home and on the road.

How it works

Three steps

  1. 01

    Ask

    Type the question in plain English.

  2. 02

    Run

    Dime writes the SQL and runs it against your data.

  3. 03

    Check

    The answer comes back with the table and the query.
